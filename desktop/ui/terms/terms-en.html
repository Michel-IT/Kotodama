4. How Kotodama uses providers' pages

For each service you turn on, Kotodama opens its official page in an internal window, where you sign in with your own account. When you press Enter or a shortcut, Kotodama types your question into the chosen pages, sends it and reads the answer to show it to you; at your request it may use the page's controls (regenerate, read aloud, attachments) and save the answer and audio on your computer. It may work with pages out of view. Every send starts from an action of yours, and the frequency is limited. You can turn services on one by one or all together; either way the choice applies to each selected service and you can withdraw it at any time.

For some services (today Qwen), the message is technically sent by simulating a press of the Enter key inside the page, because the page does not accept the message otherwise. This only serves to send your message, never to get around captchas or checks, and it is active only for the services you enable.
